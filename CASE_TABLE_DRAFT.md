### Case 1 — Karachi, Aug 2020 (corrected)
- **What changed:** original draft cited "NDMA SITREP-070" — that document is dated 3 September 2020 and covers the period 2–3 Sept (later dewatering operations), not the Aug 22–29 flood peak. Wrong document. Also corrected death toll from "27-41" to the final confirmed 41.
- **Source used now:** Dawn, "10 more die as major parts of Karachi remain powerless a day after record rain," published 28 Aug 2020 — https://www.dawn.com/news/1576798 (contemporaneous reporting: 223.5mm rain, death toll at 40 as of day 3 of the spell)
- **Cross-check:** Wikipedia "2020 Karachi floods" summary page confirms dates 24–27 Aug 2020, 41 deaths, 484mm for August (highest since 1931) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Karachi_floods
- **Still open:** need the actual NDMA daily SITREP dated ~27–29 Aug 2020 for an official (not press) death/rainfall figure. Haven't located the exact PDF yet — do this before freezing.

### Case 2 — Nawabshah/SBA, Aug 2022 (confirmed, resolved)
- **Damage figures — exact match, official statement:** Tribune, published 24 Aug 2022, directly quoting Sindh Information Minister Sharjeel Memon: "in Shaheed Benazirabad Division, 54,962 houses were partially destroyed, 23,000 completely destroyed and 696 cattle lost." — https://tribune.com.pk/story/2372885/pmd-forecasts-heavy-rains-in-parts-of-three-provinces
- **Rainfall — found:** NDMA Monsoon 2022 Daily SITREP No. 073, dated 25 Aug 2022, station rainfall table for the 24–25 Aug 24h window: **Shaheed Benazirabad 137mm** — https://www.ndma.gov.pk/storage/sitreps/August2022/0K4OF8fblju6B0XAjBr1.pdf
- **Caveat to note in the table, not hide:** the damage figure is a snapshot as of ~23–24 Aug; the rainfall reading is from the 24–25 Aug window — one day later, same ongoing flood spell (started 17 Aug), not the identical 24h period. Label the case date range as "23–25 Aug 2022" to reflect this rather than implying a single clean 24h event.
- **Resolves the earlier conflict:** this snapshot (54,962/23,000) is different from PDMA's much later final province-wide tally (72,868/66,734, dated 24 Oct 2022 — https://pdma.gos.pk/Documents/Flood/Flood_2022/Flood%20Inundation%20GIS%20Maps/Houses%20Damaged-Province%20Status-24-10-2022.pdf). Both are real; this case now explicitly uses the Aug snapshot and cites it as such.
